Common questions

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. Mazing Contest is solo and works fully offline. Ads and purchases need a connection, but the game does not.

How do I remove ads?

Buy the one-time remove-ads purchase in the game. Ads are optional rewarded ads only, and are never forced. Once you own it, the game does not start the ad SDKs and does not show the consent form.

I reinstalled or changed phones. How do I get my purchase back?

Open Settings and choose Restore purchases while signed in to the same Google Play account that bought it.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your device. There is no cloud save, so uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes your progress.

How do I change my ad consent choice?

Open Settings > Privacy options. This row appears in regions where a privacy options entry is required, including if you own Remove ads. A change applies to the next ad; ad SDKs that already started keep running until you close and reopen the game. See the privacy policy for more.

I want a refund

Purchases are handled by Google Play. Refund requests go through Google Play's own refund process; email me if you need help.

An ad did not load or did not give a reward

Ads depend on your connection and ad availability. Try again later. If a reward you earned did not arrive, email me with the time and level.
